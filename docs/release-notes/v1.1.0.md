Vinyl 1.1.0 makes every scene resizable, gives the tonearm a proper cueing animation and turns the mini player into a small turntable.

## What's new

- **Resize anything.**
    - Every desktop scene has a **Size** slider, in Settings › Desktop and in the menu bar panel. The top-down Turntable goes from a small deck in the middle of your desk to one that fills most of the screen. The Listening Room and After Hours zoom smoothly from the whole desk to a close-up of the turntable. Each scene remembers its own size.
    - The mini player resizes too. Drag its bottom-right corner (a grip appears when you hover), pinch on a trackpad, or use the Size slider. It keeps its proportions and can go from 140 to 720 points tall.
- **A tonearm that moves like a real one.** When you press play, the arm lifts off its rest, swings over the record and lowers gently into the lead-in groove. When you skip to another track or seek, it lifts, moves to the new spot and drops again. When you pause, it lifts and swings back to its rest. While a song plays, it still creeps inward through the grooves.
- **The mini player is now a small turntable.** Before, its tonearm floated over the record on its own. Now it's a complete little deck: platter, record, and an arm on its own base that cues on and off the record. If you'd rather have just the record, pick **Record Only** under Style in Settings › Mini Player, in the menu bar panel, or by right-clicking the player.

## Changes

- The parked tonearm now sits clear of the speed knob and power light.
- Settings from 1.0 carry over:
    - Wide, Mid and Close framing become the matching sizes.
    - The mini player's size and Tonearm setting become its height and style. Tonearm on gives the Turntable style, and Tonearm off gives Record Only.

## Install

Download `Vinyl.dmg`, open it and drag Vinyl to Applications. This build is signed ad hoc and not notarized, so the first time you open it, macOS will block it:

1. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**.
2. Scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to the message about Vinyl.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel.
